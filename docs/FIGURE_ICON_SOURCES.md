# Figure 1 icon sources

Figure 1 uses vector symbols to identify the ten model families in the benchmark.
Brand marks were checked against the official pages below on October 9, 2026.
They identify the evaluated models; they do not indicate affiliation or endorsement.
Provider marks are used for GPT, Llama and Command, which are model families rather
than separate organizations. The model roster and experiment results are unchanged.

| Family | Mark and reference | Vector artwork |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qwen | Current blue symbol on [Qwen](https://qwen.ai/) | [QwenLM official repository](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-MM-Plugins/blob/main/src/capabilities/video-edit/skill/assets/images/qwen-icon.svg) |
| GPT | OpenAI Blossom from the [OpenAI brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) | `OAI_OpenAI-Blossom_Black.svg` in the [official logo package](https://cdn.openai.com/brand/openai-logos.zip); artwork canvas fitted with clear space |
| Grok | Grok mark on the [official brand page](https://x.ai/legal/brand-guidelines) | SVG linked to Grok in that page's footer |
| DeepSeek | Whale mark from [DeepSeek's official repository](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-LLM/blob/main/images/logo.svg) | Lobe Icons `deepseek-color.svg` |
| Gemini | Multicolor spark on [Gemini](https://gemini.google/about/) | SVG in the official page's footer |
| Llama | Meta mark on the [official developer site](https://dev.meta.ai/) | Lobe Icons `meta-color.svg` |
| Kimi | K and blue dot shown in [Kimi's official app icon](https://www.kimi.com/pwa-192.png) | Lobe Icons `kimi.svg`, using the blue dot on a light background |
| Cogito | Polyhedral mark on [Deep Cogito](https://www.deepcogito.com/) | Lobe Icons `deepcogito-color.svg` |
| GLM | Z.ai mark on [Z.ai](https://chat.z.ai/) | [Official SVG](https://z-cdn.chatglm.cn/z-ai/static/logo.svg) |
| Command | Cohere mark in the [official site icon](https://cohere.com/favicon-32x32.png) | Lobe Icons `commanda-color.svg` |

The Lobe Icons artwork comes from commit
`c385b2b8d1f9e19aa86e628d4e23c91ee1111a47` of
[lobehub/lobe-icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons).
Its [MIT notice](../assets/figures/MODEL_ICONS_LICENSE.txt) is included.
Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.

The general diagram pictograms use [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide)
outline icons, with an added alert symbol on the answer document. The
[Lucide notices](../assets/figures/DIAGRAM_ICONS_LICENSE.txt) are included.
The small score bars and clustering dots are vector illustrations, not additional
experimental observations.
