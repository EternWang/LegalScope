How scoring works

Models receive the question or case prompt. An automatic evaluator scores their answers using separate references.

Exam answers
0–4 points for matching the reference answer’s issue, rule, application and conclusion.
Case answers
English prompt → Chinese answer → GPT-5.5 English translation → three rubric scores, each from 0 to 4.
A

Citation relevance

Does the cited law support the legal issue and proposition?

B

Constraint extraction

Does the answer follow the assigned stance, facts and instructions?

C

Argument validity

Does the conclusion follow from the law and facts?

Results report model averages on a 0–100 scale; the case mean averages A, B and C. Human validation covers 80 exam questions and 10 case prompts across all 28 configurations. Two lawyers independently rate the same 280 case answers.
