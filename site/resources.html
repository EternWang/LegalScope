The benchmark evaluates 861 exam questions and 276 case prompts. The public downloads are listed below.

Model performance and roster28 eachCSV · Parquet

Case models answered in Chinese. GPT-5.5 translated the answers into English, and the English translations were scored. The released case responses are these English translations.

Project-authored case annotations use MIT. Victorian Bar excerpts retain CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and their source notices. Original judgments and the remaining 783 exam inputs are outside the release. See the data card for scope and intended use.
